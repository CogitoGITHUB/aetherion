* What the owner asked for (item 25, 2026-10-22)

"The agent acts with no approval prompt, and also while no screen is attached: the holder
runs the agent's own words, not the screen's; the secrets still never reach an agent."
Done when: an agent adds a tab, splits a pane, closes one and sends to a shell with no
screen attached and with no token, and a secret is still refused by name on the way to an
agent. It replaces the token gate and the attached screen of item 23.

* What stands today (read from the code, 2026-10-31)

- =multiplexer_send= already talks to the holder with no screen and no view: the bytes go
  straight to a pane's shell.
- =multiplexer_control= sends =op: control= with words + the human's token. The holder
  compares the token itself (=token_matches=, =holder.rs:1667=), then forwards the words to
  the attached screen (=ask_screen=, =holder.rs:1701=) and waits for =control-result=. With
  no screen the holder refuses: "no screen is attached to this session, so there is nothing
  to drive".
- =multiplexer_view= is the same forward-to-the-screen path, so it also needs a screen.
- The words themselves are the screen's code (=control::run= → =Screen::run_command=), which
  owns the tabs, the panes, the ring of copies, the selection, the scroll and the panels.
  The holder owns the panes' processes, the layout, the log and the state record.

* What has to change on the wire

1. =op: control= is answered by the holder: no forwarding, no =control-result= from a
   screen, and the answer comes back on the same connection that asked.
2. The =confirm= field goes away, and so does the token check in the holder and in the MCP
   (=token_given=, =agents/mcp.rs:193=). =capabilities.json= says of both mutating tools
   "it needs the human's token": that sentence goes, and the declaration is re-checked.
3. =op: view= is answered by the holder when no screen is attached (the panes, their tabs,
   their lines from the log). With a screen attached it stays the screen's answer, because
   the ring of copies is the screen's.
4. Everything else stays: =attach=, =detach=, =state=, =read=, =send=, =open=, =resize=,
   =rename=, =close=, and the record of every change as the log's own record.
5. The redaction seam does not move: a line that names a credential keeps its name and loses
   its value on the way to an agent, and that stays a refusal by name.

* The one choice the owner has to make

The words fall in two groups, and the second group is where a screen is still needed:

- The session's words: =tab new=, =tab close=, =pane new= (the split), =pane close=,
  =focus PANE=, =send=. The holder already owns every object behind them, so it can run
  them with no screen and no view.
- The view's words: =scroll up/down=, =copy PANE FIRST LAST=, =paste PANE=, =paste agent=,
  =view=. They act on the ring of copies and on a scroll offset, which live in the screen.

(a) MINIMAL — the holder runs the session's words; the view's words answer by name
    ("the scroll and the ring are the screen's; item 63 moves them into the holder").
    Satisfies item 25's done-when exactly. No state is built twice, and item 63 (independent
    views per client) then moves the view state once, for the right reason.
(b) HOLDER-OWNS-THE-VIEW — the holder grows its own ring and a scroll offset per pane now,
    and runs every word with no screen. Bigger, and item 63 will rewrite the same state per
    client, so part of it is built to be thrown away.
(c) DO IT AFTER 63 — item 25 waits for the per-client views, and the agent's words stay
    screen-bound until then.

Recommendation: (a). It is the smallest true reading of item 25, it needs no second copy of
the view, and it leaves the view state to item 63, which is where the owner already ordered
it to live.
