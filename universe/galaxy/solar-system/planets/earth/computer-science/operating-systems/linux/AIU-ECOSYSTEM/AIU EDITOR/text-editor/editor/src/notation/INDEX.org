* Why this area exists
# the one paragraph a reader needs.
The AIU NOTATION engine is the notation crate, linked into the hot path, so a
page is parsed in the editor with no second program and no environment
variable. This area reads the document the seam answers and turns it into what
the commands need: the blocks with their byte offsets, the faults with their
lines, and the text a person reads.
